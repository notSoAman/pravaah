PRAVAAH Journal — Writing Guide
1. Write entirely in Notion

You don't need to know:

HTML
Markdown
programming
website design

Just write your article normally.

2. Use normal Notion formatting

You can use:

headings
paragraphs
bold
italic
links
quotes
bullet lists
numbered lists
tables
images
YouTube embeds
Vimeo embeds
3. Images

Add photographs directly to your Notion article.

Use good-quality photographs and make sure PRAVAAH has permission to publish them.

4. Videos

For videos, use:

YouTube or Vimeo embeds.

Do not upload large video files directly into the article.

5. Avoid special Notion features

For V1, don't use:

databases
toggles
synced blocks
Notion pages embedded inside articles
interactive widgets
complicated third-party embeds
forms
anything that behaves more like an application than an article

The reason isn't that Notion can't handle them.

It's because our website needs to turn the article into a predictable web page.
